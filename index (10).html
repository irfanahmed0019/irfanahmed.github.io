The Philosophy

Vibe coding done right

Built 5+ real projects, not counting the half-finished ones. Learned the hard way what matters and what's just noise.

The playbook: use Clerk, not DIY auth. Use Stripe, not custom payments. Use Tailwind + shadcn, not raw CSS. Ship the feature that matters.

Every hour saved on boilerplate is an hour that goes into the UI, the UX, the thing that makes someone say "ok this is actually sick."
